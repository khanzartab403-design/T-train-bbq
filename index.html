Low and slow. Every time.

T Train BBQ started with one smoker, a pile of hardwood, and a simple rule: no shortcuts. Brisket goes on the fire before the sun comes up and comes off only when it's ready, usually twelve hours later.

We keep the recipes simple and the ingredients honest: a coarse dry rub, real wood smoke, and sauces made in-house. The food speaks for itself, so we let it.
